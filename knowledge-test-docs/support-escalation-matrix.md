# Support Escalation

Who handles what, and when a question moves up. Owned by the Service Desk.

## Tiers

### Tier 1

The first point of contact. Handles scheduling, account questions, password resets, billing
statement queries, and anything with a documented procedure. Tier 1 answers from the
knowledge base and does not interpret clinical information.

Target: resolve at first contact, or escalate within fifteen minutes.

### Tier 2

Handles anything without a documented procedure, all clinical questions, insurance disputes,
and any request to reverse a charge older than sixty days. Tier 2 owns the answer until it is
resolved and is responsible for writing up anything that recurs.

Target: first response within four business hours.

### Tier 3

Vendor and engineering issues. Reached only through Tier 2, never directly from Tier 1.

## When to escalate immediately

Escalate to Tier 2 without attempting an answer when any of the following is true.

- The question is clinical, including anything about medication, dose or interaction.
- The caller is reporting a privacy incident or a suspected breach.
- The caller is a lawyer, a regulator, or a journalist.
- The request involves deleting or altering a clinical record.
- You cannot find a documented answer and the caller is waiting on a decision.

## Writing it up

Anything escalated to Tier 2 that took more than one exchange to resolve should end with a
knowledge base entry. The person who resolved it writes the draft; an administrator reviews
and publishes it. A question that has been escalated three times without an article is a
process failure, not a knowledge gap.
